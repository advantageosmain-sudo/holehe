Privacy & limitations

Use registration signals responsibly.

What this site does

Static hosting serves documentation only. The optional local form sends the configured address to the loopback backend, which makes requests to selected third-party services. The app does not store lookup history or recovery details. The CLI also runs on your own machine.

Interpret results

Account recovery behavior varies by service and changes over time. Rate limits, unavailable modules, or ambiguous responses can make results unreliable. Do not treat a signal as proof that a particular person controls an account.

Respect people and services

Use the tool only for addresses you have a legitimate reason to check. Review applicable service rules and avoid unwanted investigation of others.
