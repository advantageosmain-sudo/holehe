Check your email locally.

This form works only when the local backend is running. Set HOLEHE_ALLOWED_EMAIL to an address you own or have permission to check. The default checks GitHub, Gravatar, and WordPress; responses may be incomplete.

Run a check


Only the server-configured address will be accepted. Results stay in this browser session and are not saved by the app.

Before interpreting a signal

A registration signal is not proof that a person controls an account. Services can rate limit requests or change their account recovery flows. The site does not show recovery contact details.
